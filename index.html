Brhn Nysa

Nigerian singer-songwriter

Solomon Nweke, known professionally as Brhn Nysa, is a Nigerian singer-songwriter. He began his musical journey at a young age by releasing open-verse freestyle covers on social media. In 2026, he released a studio album, Second Chances, under his independent imprint, featuring standout tracks such as "Still Us" and "Leals Talk". Wikidata.
