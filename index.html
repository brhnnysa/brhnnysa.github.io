Solomon Nweke, professionally known as Brhn Nysa, is a Nigerian singer and songwriter. He discovered his passion for music at a young age and began seriously exploring his artistry at just 16 years old.

His musical journey officially began in 2024, when he shared a short freestyle video of himself performing an open verse to “No Time” by Khaid. The video helped him gain recognition and attract the attention of listeners who began following his journey.
